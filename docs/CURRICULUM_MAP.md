# Curriculum map and placement contract

**Version:** 1.0 — 23 September 2026. Adapted from the supplied `syllabus_map.md`, with corrections recorded in [C1_FILES_REVIEW.md](C1_FILES_REVIEW.md). This is an authoring map, not a claim that the lessons have been written.

Read with [COURSE_SPEC.md](COURSE_SPEC.md) and [BUILD_SPEC.md](../BUILD_SPEC.md). Preserve these stable IDs when titles change. Lesson IDs append `-L01`, `-L02`, and so on; practice and assessment versions have separate identities. Dependencies below identify required objectives, which may be satisfied by evidence-backed placement waivers. They do not force repetition of an entire mastered module. Short repairs below B2 use `P-001` onward and link to the affected objective.

Every row needs authored explanations, vocabulary, relevant visuals, practice, feedback, an unfamiliar exit task, and a published success rubric. Example lengths and numerical exit thresholds are project coaching choices, not official DTB word limits or certification criteria. Passing an exit check supplies evidence; the separate-day and delayed-transfer requirements in `mastery-v1` still apply. Provide an alternative exit task for a retest rather than repeating the answer just taught.

## Diagnostic: short, resumable rounds

| ID | Evidence to collect | Provisional time | Result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| D1 | 15 mixed items covering gender/case, adjective/noun endings, verbs, word order and connectors; five independently composed workplace sentences | 20 min | Objective-level grammar findings, initial bridge routing |
| D2 | Original workplace text with gist, detail, inference, vocabulary-in-context questions and a short paraphrase | 20 min | Reading and lexical evidence; distinguish misreading from missing vocabulary |
| D3 | Complaint reply, approximately 150 words, plus a short justified workplace opinion | 25 min | Task fulfillment, structure, register, grammar and range; retain the actual texts |
| D4 | Recorded two-minute workplace monologue plus spontaneous follow-ups and a clarification exchange | 15 min | Speaking evidence; assess sound-dependent dimensions only from accessible audio |
| D5 | Original playable conversation and phone message; gist/detail questions plus a short note of names, numbers and actions | 10–15 min | Listening and listening-to-writing evidence; transcript hidden during the attempt |

Round durations are divisible, not a first-session requirement. Begin teaching after D1; finish other rounds across early sessions. D1's task counts do not establish broad competence without adequate objective coverage. An unavailable scorer leaves that evidence pending; absent audio leaves the modality unassessed. Never infer listening from D2 or spoken fluency from a typed D4 substitute.

Record one route per bridge module: `skip` for redundant teaching with adequate diagnostic evidence; `check` for uncertainty requiring a transfer task; `repair` for targeted gaps; `full` for broad instructional need. Include reason, evidence IDs, waived objectives, and date. A route is not a mastery state. A short diagnostic cannot establish delayed retention.

Provisionally budget 2–4 study sessions per full module, with more if needed and much less when placement calls for a check or repair. Re-estimate after observed sessions rather than announcing a fixed completion date.

The B2-12 checkpoint includes 20 mixed grammar items (coaching target 16 correct), one email meeting the bridge rubric, an unfamiliar reading task, an actual listening task, and a three-minute voice task with follow-ups. Use B2-11's 7/10 comprehension targets. Recommend C1 entry from the resulting profile, not a single total. A missing modality remains a named repair/assessment need; the learner can start suitable C1 work while resolving it.

M1 publishes the six lessons identified in BUILD_SPEC, with inline prerequisite support. M2 completes this bridge. Diagnostic placement may let the learner skip those lessons; that does not remove the requirement to author them.

## Part I: professional C1

Publish all eighteen modules and at least 48 substantial lessons overall. Provisionally allow 3–5 sessions per full module; use observed pace and coverage to expand. Mix technology, software, e-commerce and entrepreneurship examples with other workplace sectors. Every lesson supplies its own input and lexical support.

Use a separate C1 course rubric for task fulfillment, organization/cohesion, register, accuracy and range, adding interaction, mediation or intelligibility where appropriate. Define observable target descriptors and acceptable alternatives before publishing a task. Do not silently turn every lesson into an exam-score exercise.

Recommend Part II from this evidence; keep exam practice accessible if the learner chooses to start earlier. Record gaps rather than changing assessment history.

## Parallel article and pronunciation tracks

| ID | Focus | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| ART-01 | Gender versus case; feminine singular/plural; full noun packages | Identify gender and case in 20 contextual phrases and explain three choices; allow syncretic forms where context is insufficient |
| ART-02 | True suffixes, scope and look-alike endings | Apply taught reliable patterns to 30 examples, at least 28 correct; explain why look-alikes are excluded |
| ART-03 | Tendencies, meaning groups and uncertainty | Classify 20 cases as reliable rule/tendency/lookup-needed and verify lexical forms; no invented confidence percentages |
| ART-04 | Compound heads, plural patterns and sense-dependent gender | At least 19/20 compounds with identified heads and correct noun packages |
| ART-05 | Personal article review and independent use | Native review queue from confirmed errors; later transfer evidence resolves patterns; a small deck alone is not progress |
| PR-01 | Vowel length and umlauts | Actual recorded contrasts and new sentence use; compare meaning and intelligibility |
| PR-02 | ich/ach sounds, final devoicing and consonant contrasts | Actual audio in words and connected speech; feedback supported by the analysis capability |
| PR-03 | Stress in compounds, prefixes and borrowed words | Mark then record stress in unfamiliar examples |
| PR-04 | Sentence focus, rhythm and intonation | Express different intended meanings in recorded utterances |
| PR-05 | Shadowing, chunking and spontaneous fluency | Shadow a supplied clip, then explain new material without reading; evaluate actual sound |

ART-02 follows ART-01; ART-03/04 build on ART-02. ART-05 runs throughout. PR progression depends on observed need. An audio-capable evaluator can supply qualified coaching evidence; an optional human review is additional evidence with provenance. Neither a transcript nor written pronunciation advice establishes sound-based competence. No external partner is compulsory.

## After C1: C2 starter pathway

`C2-00` is a fresh diagnostic, followed by at least four substantial starter lessons:

| ID | Focus | Performance task |
|---|---|---|
| C2-01 | Synthesis and source disagreement | Combine supplied sources while separating claims, evidence and uncertainty |
| C2-02 | Nuance, implication and register | Reframe the same sensitive message for contrasting audiences and explain the choices |
| C2-03 | Advanced argument and persuasion | Defend a position, respond to a strong objection and revise it when warranted |
| C2-04 | Complex listening and spontaneous discussion | Interpret implied attitudes in actual audio and sustain a responsive discussion |

These are a starter route and new performance goals, not a complete C2 course or proof of C2. Select any future C2 examination separately; there is no DTB C2 target in this project.
