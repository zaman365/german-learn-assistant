# Twelve speaking themes: prepare ideas, not a script

These are original preparation notes for the public DTB C1 topic areas. They are not official task sheets. Choose a position, support it with a concrete example, acknowledge a limitation, and answer the question actually asked. A useful two-minute structure is **context → two developed points → example → qualified conclusion**. The timing is a practice target; there is no invented minimum word count.

Use three keyword lines, then close them and speak. Repeat with a different angle. For a follow-up, begin with a direct answer before adding background. For partner mediation, preserve the other person's position and conditions rather than substituting your own opinion.

| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |

## 1. A career-shaping decision

Original prompt: You moved from solving individual support cases to improving the support process. Explain the decision, an alternative you considered and what changed. A useful contrast is **Damals war mir … wichtig; inzwischen lege ich mehr Wert auf …**. Separate an outcome from an expectation: **Ich erhoffte mir …; tatsächlich …**. Avoid claiming that one choice guarantees success.

Follow-ups: Welche Nachteile haben Sie in Kauf genommen? Was würden Sie mit Ihrem heutigen Wissen anders entscheiden? Partner check: summarize the decisive reason, then the reservation your partner mentioned.

## 2. Education or vocational training

Explain how one practical assignment helped you apply theory. Contrast **Wissen erwerben** with **Kenntnisse anwenden**. Include a gap in the training and how you addressed it; a purely chronological list does not explain its value. Use **Während der Ausbildung …; erst im Berufsalltag …** to compare stages.

Follow-ups: Welche Fähigkeit hätten Sie gern früher gelernt? Wie lässt sich der Lernerfolg im Betrieb erkennen? Partner check: distinguish a qualification from the concrete skill it helped develop.

## 3. A service and its customer value

Describe a repair-booking service: its users, the problem it solves, the steps and a limitation. Explain value from the customer's perspective with **Der Nutzen besteht darin, dass …**. A fast confirmation is different from a guaranteed repair date. Include how customers can ask questions when the standard process does not fit.

Follow-ups: Für wen wäre dieser Service ungeeignet? Woran würden Sie eine Verbesserung erkennen? Partner check: preserve both the claimed benefit and the condition under which it applies.

## 4. How a product is made

Explain a small furniture manufacturer's process from measurement to final inspection. Make actors, sequence and quality checks explicit: **Nachdem …, wird …; bevor …, prüft …**. Explain one technical word in everyday German. Mention what happens when the final check reveals a defect.

Follow-ups: An welcher Stelle sind Fehler besonders teuer? Was könnte automatisiert werden, und was braucht weiterhin menschliche Beurteilung? Partner check: retain the order and the exception, not every minor step.

## 5. Effective leadership

Contrast clear expectations with constant detailed control. Use an example involving a missed deadline and an early warning. **Einerseits braucht das Team …; andererseits …** should introduce a real tension. A strong leader can explain a decision and revise it when new evidence appears.

Follow-ups: Wie gehen Sie mit einer berechtigten Kritik um? Wann muss eine Führungskraft selbst entscheiden? Partner check: identify the quality your partner values and the example that supports it.

## 6. Your own business idea

Propose a service that helps small retailers maintain product information. Explain customers, revenue, initial costs and the assumption most in need of testing. Say **Unter der Voraussetzung, dass …** instead of presenting a hoped-for market as an established fact. A small pilot should answer a defined question.

Follow-ups: Was wäre Ihr erster kostengünstiger Test? Unter welchen Bedingungen würden Sie die Idee aufgeben? Partner check: preserve the target group and the most important uncertainty.

## 7. Self-employment and salaried work

Compare autonomy, income predictability, responsibility and access to support using the same criteria. **Im Vergleich zu …** and **Das hängt davon ab, ob …** help avoid blanket claims. A salaried job can also involve autonomy; self-employment does not remove dependence on customers.

Follow-ups: Welche Form passt in welcher Lebensphase? Wie würden Sie mit schwankenden Einnahmen umgehen? Partner check: report a conditional preference without turning it into a universal recommendation.

## 8. Why a company succeeds

Discuss a fictional repair company that receives repeat orders. Consider reliable communication, quality, staffing and financial planning. Separate evidence from an interpretation: **Die erneuten Aufträge sprechen dafür, dass …; sie beweisen jedoch nicht …**. Show how the factors interact rather than listing slogans.

Follow-ups: Kann ein schnell wachsendes Unternehmen trotzdem Probleme haben? Welche Kennzahl würde Ihre Einschätzung ergänzen? Partner check: distinguish the observed result from its possible cause.

## 9. Building customer loyalty

Explain how a business can maintain trust after a mistake. Include acknowledgement, a realistic remedy, a named next action and learning from the case. **Kundenbindung** is broader than a discount. Avoid making compensation promises you have no authority to give.

Follow-ups: Was tun Sie bei einer unerfüllbaren Forderung? Wie unterscheiden Sie einen Einzelfall von einem wiederkehrenden Problem? Partner check: retain the remedy and its limit.

## 10. An app useful at work

Describe a shared task app from a colleague's perspective. Explain one workflow, one benefit and one risk without technical jargon. **Die Anwendung ermöglicht es uns, …; Voraussetzung ist allerdings …** connects function and condition. A new tool may initially create extra work because responsibilities and data need checking.

Follow-ups: Welche Alternative gibt es für Personen ohne Zugang? Woran erkennen Sie nach vier Wochen, ob die App hilft? Partner check: describe the task the app supports rather than reciting its features.

## 11. A successful advertising campaign

Invent a small campaign for a repair workshop. Distinguish audience reach, inquiries and confirmed bookings. State what you can and cannot infer from the numbers. **Die Reichweite stieg, während …; deshalb würden wir zunächst …** creates a reasoned comparison. Avoid calling a campaign successful solely because many people saw it.

Follow-ups: Welche andere Erklärung könnte den Anstieg erklären? Wie würden Sie eine zweite Kampagne vergleichen? Partner check: keep the metric and denominator your partner used.

## 12. A company's social media use

Discuss customer questions, useful information and complaints on a public channel. Distinguish public explanation from private case details. Set responsibilities and response windows. **Wir würden öffentlich erläutern, …; personenbezogene Einzelheiten klären wir …** is a useful contrast. Consider staffing and moderation as well as reach.

Follow-ups: Was tun Sie bei einer sachlich falschen Kritik? Welche Aufgaben gehören nicht in diesen Kanal? Partner check: preserve the boundary between general information and the individual case.

## Worked partner explanation

Partner contribution: **Ich wäre für die App, wenn auch die Spätschicht Zugang erhält. Sonst entstehen zwei verschiedene Listen.**

Accurate mediation: **Meine Partnerin unterstützt die Einführung unter der Bedingung, dass beide Schichten damit arbeiten können. Sie möchte parallele Listen vermeiden.**

Misleading mediation: **Meine Partnerin will die App sofort für alle einführen.** This removes the condition and adds an immediate commitment. A stronger follow-up asks how access would be organized; it does not repeat the whole prepared presentation.

Independent practice: Choose a theme you have not used today. Record two minutes, answer both follow-ups without notes, then explain the supplied partner contribution in different words. Assess task coverage, coherent development, responsive answers and accurate mediation. Sound-dependent feedback requires the recording; a typed outline alone supplies no speaking or pronunciation evidence.
