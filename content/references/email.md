# A professional email that gets a useful response

Start with the recipient's need. State why you are writing, give the relevant facts, propose the next step and say which response you need. A subject line should identify the matter: **Lieferung 482: aktualisierter Versandtermin**.

| Function        | Usable phrasing                                     | Limit                                            |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Acknowledge     | Vielen Dank für Ihre Rückmeldung.                   | Be specific if there is a complaint.             |
| Regret          | Wir bedauern die Verzögerung.                       | Do not invent an admission or a cause.           |
| Confirm         | Wir bestätigen den Versand am Donnerstag.           | Confirm only what is actually known.             |
| Indicate a plan | Der Versand ist für Donnerstag vorgesehen.          | A plan is not a guarantee.                       |
| Request         | Bitte teilen Sie uns mit, ob …                      | State exactly what you need to know.             |
| Offer help      | Für Rückfragen stehen wir Ihnen gern zur Verfügung. | Use when it meaningfully supports the next step. |

Keep **Sie/Ihnen/Ihr** consistent in formal correspondence. **Sehr geehrte Frau Kaya,** is followed by a lowercase continuation unless the next word requires a capital independently. End with **Mit freundlichen Grüßen** without a comma in normal German correspondence.

Separate dispatch (**Versand**) from arrival (**Zustellung/Ankunft**). Preserve uncertainty with **voraussichtlich**, **vorgesehen**, or a clear limit such as **können wir noch nicht bestätigen**. Do not translate a blunt internal note literally into a customer reply.

Before sending, check: recipient, requested points, facts versus assumptions, dates/numbers, tone, sentence structure, article/adjective agreement, commas and the requested action. More complex language is useful only when it improves precision or communication.

Practice: replace **Schreiben Sie uns bitte** with a specific request about Friday delivery. One answer: **Bitte teilen Sie uns mit, ob eine voraussichtliche Ankunft am Freitag für Sie infrage kommt.**
