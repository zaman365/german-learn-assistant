# Mediation: preserve meaning for a new audience

Mediation selects, explains and reorganizes information for someone else's purpose. It can be German-to-German; it is not limited to translation.

1. Identify the audience's task and prior knowledge.
2. Extract the necessary facts, conditions, exceptions and uncertainty.
3. Separate the source's findings, recommendations and reported opinions.
4. Rephrase in suitable language, defining an unfamiliar term in place.
5. Check every claim against the source. Label missing information rather than filling it in.

**Internal:** „Reklamation berechtigt; Ersatz nur nach Prüfung des Fotos.“ **Customer-facing:** „Es tut uns leid, dass die Ware beschädigt angekommen ist. Bitte senden Sie uns ein Foto, damit wir den Schaden prüfen und den Ersatz veranlassen können.“ Preserve the condition; do not promise unconditional replacement if the instruction does not permit it.

Attribution phrases have different strength: **laut Bericht / nach Angaben der Leitung / die Untersuchung legt nahe / daraus geht nicht hervor**. **Angeblich** can suggest doubt or distance; it is not a neutral substitute for every source marker.

A usable phone note contains caller, callback details, reason, relevant numbers/dates, requested action and urgency. Do not transcribe every hesitation. Do preserve a negation such as **nicht vor Donnerstag**. If a digit is unclear, mark it for confirmation instead of silently inventing it.
