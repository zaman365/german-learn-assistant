# Numbers, dates and comparisons

| Meaning                    | German                       | Check                                               |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Change from 40 to 50       | Anstieg **um** 10 **auf** 50 | um = amount changed; auf = final level              |
| Rate moves from 20% to 25% | plus 5 Prozentpunkte         | Relative increase = 25%, not 5%.                    |
| Decimal                    | 2,5 Stunden                  | German decimal comma; spoken zwei Komma fünf.       |
| Deadline                   | spätestens am 15. Oktober    | Includes the named day, subject to any stated time. |
| Period                     | vom 3. bis zum 7. Mai        | A range, not two isolated appointments.             |
| Calendar date              | am vierzehnten Mai           | Dative ending after am.                             |
| Quantity approximation     | knapp 100 / gut 100          | A little below / a little above; context matters.   |

German two-digit number order can cause errors: **einundvierzig** = 41, **vierzehn** = 14. Telephone numbers are often read in short digit groups. Ask for confirmation rather than relying on a guessed grouping. **Eineinhalb** means one and a half; **anderthalb** is an alternative. Write a unit with each number where confusion is possible.

Compare like with like. A monthly total and a weekly average cannot support a direct trend claim without normalization. A rise in complaints can reflect a higher number of customers rather than a higher complaint rate. Say what the data measure, over which period, and with what limitation.

Useful language: **stieg/sank von … auf …; blieb weitgehend stabil; erreichte einen Höchststand; gegenüber dem Vorjahr; lässt sich aus den vorliegenden Daten nicht ableiten.** Use an exact figure for an exact question and an approximation only when appropriate.
